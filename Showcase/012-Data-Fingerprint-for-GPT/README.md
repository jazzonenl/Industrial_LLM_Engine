## Overview
**Industrial LLM Engine** is a next-generation platform now enhanced with our proprietary **State Signature Technology** — a universal layer for applying large language models (LLMs) in complex industrial environments.
It transforms raw multi-sensor telemetry into compact, stable, and comparable state representations that GPT-5 and other AI systems can instantly interpret, compare, and recall.

This technology is already in **real-world use** — analyzing equipment telemetry in real time, detecting operational deviations, and delivering meaningful explanations in natural language tailored for engineers, operators, and decision-makers.

We have successfully applied it in simulations and deployments across:
- **Energy** (wind turbines, power grids, transformers)
- **Microelectronics** (EUV lithography systems, semiconductor production)
- **Transportation** (automotive, rail, maritime)
- **Aerospace** (engine telemetry, satellite systems)

Domains where early detection of even the smallest deviations can prevent costly failures and ensure operational continuity.

---

## 🔍 Key Advantages

- **Deep contextual encoding**
  Each parameter is mapped into a rich vector-based *state signature* that preserves its role, scale, and interrelations within the system.

- **High sensitivity to changes**
  Even minimal fluctuations shift the signature in detectable ways, enabling identification of subtle anomalies.

- **Flexible comparison**
  Supports analysis and matching of systems with different structures — a challenge for traditional telemetry methods.

- **Integrated change signatures**
  Instead of comparing values one by one, the engine forms a holistic signature of how the system’s state evolves over time.
